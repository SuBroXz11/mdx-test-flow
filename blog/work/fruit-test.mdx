# A Tale of Two Fruits

Some of the best subjects are the ones sitting right on your counter. Today it's a simple side-by-side: one banana, and three apples sharing a plate.

## The Banana

![A ripe yellow banana on a white background](https://pub-0ab006c3369744cbaec80a266edb26da.r2.dev/fruit-test/01-banana.jpeg)

Curved, cheerfully yellow, with just a touch of brown at the stem — this banana is at peak ripeness. No bruising, no green tint left, just the kind of even color that means it's ready to eat right now. Bananas like this are the quiet workhorse of the fruit bowl: portable, no plate required, and sweet without any prep.

## The Apples

![Three red and yellow apples on a patterned plate](https://pub-0ab006c3369744cbaec80a266edb26da.r2.dev/fruit-test/02-test-img2.jpeg)

These three apples tell a different story. Each one shows that classic red-over-yellow blush — the kind you get from apples that spent plenty of time in direct sun as they grew. Resting on a plate with a leafy green rim, they look almost staged for a still life, but the appeal is real: crisp, a little tart, a little sweet, and ready for a snack or a pie.

## Two Fruits, One Lesson

Put a banana and an apple side by side and you get a small lesson in contrast — soft curve versus round form, uniform yellow versus mottled red, and two totally different textures waiting underneath the skin. Sometimes the simplest photos make the best case for eating your fruit.
